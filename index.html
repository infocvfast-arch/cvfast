Frequently asked questions

Are CVFAST CVs ATS-friendly?

Our professional services are structured with modern applicant tracking systems in mind while remaining clear and readable for recruiters.

How quickly will I receive my CV?

CV Refresh standard delivery normally takes 1–4 business days. Professional CV and CV + Cover Letter standard delivery normally take 1–3 business days. Priority Delivery can be selected during checkout for eligible human-written services.

What happens after payment?

After completing your payment, return to CVFAST and complete the Order Form. Upload your current CV and provide the information needed to process your order.

Can I choose Priority Delivery?

Yes. For eligible human-written CV services, choose Standard or Priority Delivery directly during PayPal checkout.

How will I receive my completed CV?

Your finished documents will be delivered digitally using the contact information provided with your order.

Can I contact someone before ordering?

Yes. You can contact CVFAST through WhatsApp or email before placing your order.
